Surviving Michael Myers is not about running first and thinking later. In Halloween: The Game, the win condition for the Heroes of Haddonfield has a clear order of operations: **secure the NPC civilians first, then yourself**. Collect the right items, unlock an escape route, walk every civilian out alive — that is a winning night in Haddonfield.

## Win Condition and Escape Exits

Every match, your first job is to find the special civilians in the map and get them hiding safely. From there the loop is: gather items → unlock an escape → escort everyone out.

There are **five escape exits**, each holding up to **4 people per trip**, each with its own unlock requirements:

- **Cellar** — opens with a **cellar key** or **bolt cutters**. An axe is optional here, carry one only if you find it.
- **Gate** — the strictest unlock: **fuse + axe**, plus a **gate key or bolt cutters**. All three parts, no substitutes.
- **Regular car** — needs a **car key** and **fuel**. A repair kit's role in the car escape is not fully confirmed yet; treat it as optional.
- **Police car** — currently unreliable: a known bug means it often will not drive out even when unlocked. The unlock chain itself: call the police from a phone, wait for Michael to kill the responding officer, then take the **police car key** he drops.
- **Police van** — unlock conditions are still unverified; experiment.

There is also a **final-chance timed escape** — the last window of the match. It most likely opens once most civilians have escaped or died, sometimes after police discover a body. Calling different phone numbers frequently during the match seems to improve its trigger odds.

## The Priority Order That Wins Matches

1. **Find and unlock an escape exit.**
2. **Locate the scattered survivor NPCs.**
3. **Collect the items those exits need.**

Then handle civilians by type:

- **Normal civilians:** order them to call the police, then keep searching for survivors and supplies.
- **Special civilians:** order them to hide immediately — they are worth more and Michael hunts them harder — then continue looting.

The moment any exit opens, go back and walk your civilians to it. Escaping alone is a consolation prize, not a win.

## Combat Rules: Never Brawl the Shape

Unless you are cornered with no other option, do not fight Michael head-on. **Multiple civilians escaping outranks your own survival every time.** The tools exist to buy seconds, not to win fights:

- **Flare gun and firecrackers** briefly stun him — your escape window.
- If Michael grabs you, **mash the button prompt** with a **pocket knife or ice pick** equipped to break free. The item is consumed on use.

Terrain matters too: **never sprint through grass or fallen leaves** — the noise pinpoints you, and Michael locks on fast.

One special case: the **police** playstyle ends matches outright. Deal enough damage to put Michael on the ground, complete the arrest minigame, and the match ends immediately in a win.

## The Four Character Stats

Every survivor rolls four stats that shape how they handle a night in Haddonfield:

- **Melee** — your strength in close-quarters fights.
- **Athleticism** — movement speed and stamina pool; drives long chases and map traversal.
- **Composure** — faster civilian interactions and faster police calls, plus resistance to fear.
- **Ingenuity** — how well you repair, drive, and handle guns.

## Every Survival Item, Grouped

**Life-savers (grab-breakers):**

- **Talisman** — passive version of a knife break: auto-triggers on a grab, no prompt needed.
- **Pocket knife, butterfly knife, ice pick** — mash to break a grab once, consumed after.

**Control and distraction:**

- **Flare gun / firecrackers** — stun Michael; save them for chases and door blocks.
- **Road flare** — blinds Michael and can call police support.
- **Flashlight** — brief blind, useful mid-chase.
- **Smoke** — reveals sound ripples: Michael's steps, survivor markers, noise pings. Best map awareness in an item.
- **Lantern** — lights up Michael's live position on the map. See the [purple items guide](/items/purple-items).

**Utility and healing:**

- **Bolt cutters** — melee-capable and opens cellars, gates and loot boxes; the single most flexible key item.
- **Repair kit** — fixes cars, breaker boxes; enables escape routes.
- **Hourglass** — highlights escape items within 10 m while carried; thrown, it stuns. Also covered in the [purple items guide](/items/purple-items).
- **Medical kit** — three full heals, but you must stand still to use it.
- **Medical spray** — one full heal you can use *while being chased*. The panic button.
- **Baseball / brick** — basic throwables to interrupt and distract.
- **Beer** — lowers fear but warps your vision; use with care.

**Spend discipline is the real skill:** control, break-free and heal items are match-winners only if they are still in your bag when the chase starts. Do not burn a flare gun on a quiet street.
