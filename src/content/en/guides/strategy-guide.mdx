This playbook covers both sides of a match: Michael's kill economy, the civilian rescue meta, and the scoring rules that decide who wins.

## Playing Michael: the kill economy

1. **Chase gold, not blue.** Golden dots and golden noise pings are the high-value special targets (always NPCs). Killing them is worth far more than trading blows with players.
2. **Stalk to tier 3 before every kill.** Stalk (middle mouse, then right-click zoom — it tracks much faster) raises a victim's stock; level 3 weakens them, marks them longer, and boosts your kill score. Stock decays over time, so refresh it.
3. **Prefer context executions.** Environmental kills — kitchen counters, toilets, poles, graves, fire pits, windows, gym equipment — pay more bloodthirst and XP than plain attacks. Kitchens and bathrooms are execution buffets.
4. **Run the bloodthirst economy.** Tier 3 bloodthirst means more damage dealt, less taken, and weapon executions. It drops when you stand in light, take damage, or get knocked down — so cut power boxes, work from the shadows, and disengage from groups.
5. **Manage shape jump carefully.** You can only enter or exit it in darkness and out of line of sight; it also lets you phase through unlit doors. Blackout (Ctrl+F) kills nearby lights first — and it knocks riders off bikes.
6. **Sabotage communications.** Cut or break phone lines so victims can't call the police. Broken devices force survivors to spend a repair kit to restore them.
7. **Bait with the 99 trick.** Leave a stalked victim at 99 stock — when they approach, you get a hold-E kill prompt.
8. **Watch the detainment meter.** Authority figures with flashlights fill it (three stars max). Never brawl in the open against a group with flashlights; you cannot win a straight fight against a coordinated team.
9. **Win condition:** score **3,000+ points**. Civilians are worth more than cops, so protect your kill priority.

## Playing civilian: the rescue meta

1. **Wait for a full 1v4 lobby** and pick a character with decent stats — Eric's low athleticism and poor stamina make him a bad pick; sturdier characters take no damage through windows.
2. **Prepare your perk deck** before queueing: roll perks with your points, equip the good ones (stamina reducers are top tier), scrap the junk. Equipped perks are the ones that show up in your matches.
3. **Run the rescue loop:** walk near NPCs to reveal them on your minimap, convince them to follow you (press both mouse buttons), lead them away from Michael, then **hide** them — hidden civilians are much harder for Michael to find. Give spare weapons to NPCs (R).
4. **Learn the escapes and their items:** the car needs a repair kit, gas can and sedan key; the edge-gate needs bolt cutters or a padlock key; police wagons arrive if someone phones the law; hatches and storm cellars spawn around the map. Exits have limited slots — coordinate with voice chat.
5. **Carry anti-grab items:** a butterfly knife or ice pick escapes Michael's grab once; an amulet saves you automatically. Save them for the moment he actually grabs you.
6. **Don't waste ammo mid-execution** — Michael is immune during the animation. Shove instead to interrupt and rescue.
7. **Save yourself last.** Escaping removes you from the match; every civilian saved before you leave is more team score.

## Detainment: how it actually works

Downing Michael isn't enough. Cops and Loomis must pile onto him and complete skill checks to detain him — it needs several authority figures, and flashlight beams speed the meter up. Even experienced players see successful detainments only rarely. Once dead, a Loomis stays out of the round, and revived cops keep coming.

## Scoring

Residents are worth the most points, players less, cops least. Michael wins at **3,000+ points**; otherwise the civilian side wins on points. If you die early, don't quit — spectator mini-games grant perks to your team and a chance to return.

| Side | Priority | Points |
