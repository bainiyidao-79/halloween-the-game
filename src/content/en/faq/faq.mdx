Every frequently asked question about Halloween: The Game, answered with what's officially confirmed as of September 15, 2026.

## Is the game out?

Yes — it released on **September 8, 2026**, with Digital Deluxe owners playing four days early (September 4).

## Is there a free version?

No. It's a standard paid title; Steam's China region lists it at ¥137, and pricing varies by region and edition (Standard, Digital Deluxe, and a physical Collector's Edition for PS5).

## What platforms is it on?

PC (Steam), PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.

## Is there a PS4 version?

No PS4 version has been announced in any source we checked as of September 15, 2026.

## Is it on Game Pass?

Not announced in any source we checked.

## Is there a demo?

No public demo. A pre-launch beta ran before release; that client no longer works — the full game is live.

## Does it have crossplay?

Yes, across PC, PS5 and Xbox Series. It was unreliable at launch but was fixed in **Patch 1.0.1**.

## Is there a single-player story?

Yes — "The Night He Came Home": a prologue plus five chapters where you play as Michael Myers. It takes roughly **two hours** and is replayable on higher difficulties. See our [story page](/intro/story-and-setting).

## Can you play as Michael Myers in multiplayer?

Yes — one player controls Michael against up to four survivors, with NPC civilians to hunt or protect. See [how multiplayer works](/intro/multiplayer-and-crossplay).

## Can you kill Michael?

No. Downed Michael can only be **detained** — cops and Dr. Loomis pile on and complete skill checks. It's rare and requires several players coordinating.

## Is there a physical copy?

Yes — including a physical Collector's Edition for PS5 sold through major retailers.

## Is it based on the movie?

Yes — it adapts the 1978 *Halloween* film directly. Halloween and Michael Myers are trademarks of Compass International Pictures; the game is developed by IllFonic and co-published by Gun Interactive.

## Does it have proximity chat?

No official confirmation in the sources we checked as of September 15, 2026.

## How many players is multiplayer?

1 Michael vs up to 4 civilian players, with NPC civilians to hunt, protect, rescue and evacuate.
