Halloween: The Game runs its 1v4 matches on **four maps**: Haddonfield Heights, Orange Grove, Haddonfield Town Center, and East Haddonfield. None of them use fixed coordinates — escape gates, cellars, vehicles and radios all spawn **randomly inside preset point pools** on each map. What you can learn is where those pools sit, and that knowledge decides your first three minutes every match.

## Read the Civilians Before You Route

Open the map at match start and the civilians are already marked:

- **Blue markers** — normal civilians
- **Orange markers** — special civilians, worth more

A special civilian spawning near you is a gift: **route to orange first**, then build your [escape plan](/guides/survivor-beginner-guide) around them. The same map check works when you rejoin a match as police or Dr. Loomis and have forgotten where everyone was.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/maps/escape-points-radio-spawns-4.webp" alt="The in-game map showing civilian markers in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Blue means civilian, orange means special — orange first.</figcaption></div>

## Haddonfield Heights

A residential district with **densely packed houses**. Escape doors, cars and radio spawns spread **all around the blocks**, while **cellars tend to spawn toward the map edges**. Expect short distances between loot houses and a lot of door-to-door noise discipline.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/maps/escape-points-radio-spawns-1.webp" alt="The Haddonfield Heights map overview in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Haddonfield Heights — tight blocks, cellars on the rim.</figcaption></div>

## Orange Grove

The estate map: a **large mansion and villa quarter** with buildings clustered together. **Vehicles spawn heavily along the roads**, and escape exits distribute across **all four sides of the map**. If your plan needs a car escape, this map feeds it.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/maps/escape-points-radio-spawns-2.webp" alt="The Orange Grove estate map overview in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Orange Grove — villas in the middle, cars along the roads.</figcaption></div>

## Haddonfield Town Center

The commercial strip: **mixed building types** with spawn points **spread over long distances**, and some points tucked **behind shops and buildings**. Knowledge of the back alleys pays more here than on any other map.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/maps/escape-points-radio-spawns-3.webp" alt="The Haddonfield Town Center map overview in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Town Center — wide gaps, spawns hidden behind storefronts.</figcaption></div>

## East Haddonfield

The sparse one: **few buildings, lots of open ground**. Escape points **concentrate along the map edges**, and **vehicle spawns cluster together** — one car run can service several exits.

Because spawn pools shuffle every match, the winning habit is procedural, not memorized: read the map for **orange markers**, pick the nearest **edge cellar or roadside car** pool, and only then start looting. The [items guide](/items/items-guide) lists what each exit type needs.
