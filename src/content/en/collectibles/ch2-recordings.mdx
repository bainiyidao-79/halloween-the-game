**Chapter 2 — Main Street** turns Michael loose on the town's commercial strip, with **two recordings** hidden on opposite ends of the map: Loomis Log #9 and Loomis Log #3.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch2-recordings.webp" alt="A dark street scene in Main Street in Halloween The Game" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Main Street — two recordings, both ends of town.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #9 — the A-Side Music Store

Once free exploration opens up, make for the **A-Side Music Store** — the same stop where you'll find and kill **Tanya**, so the two objectives share a route.

The recording waits **in the back of the store, on the far side** from the entrance.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch2-recordings-1.webp" alt="Back of the A-Side Music Store in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Deep in the music store — Log #9 sits at the back.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch2-recordings-2.webp" alt="The music store interior where Tanya is found in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Tanya's stop and the log are one visit.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #3 — the House by the Cemetery

Cross to the **other side of the map**, directly **north of the cemetery**, where an **optional-entry house** stands — the same one where you can catch the two friends of Rachel inside.

Inside the living room, **the recording sits on top of the fireplace**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch2-recordings-2.webp" alt="The optional house north of the cemetery in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The house north of the cemetery — optional entry, mandatory loot.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch2-recordings-3.webp" alt="Loomis Log 3 on the fireplace inside the house in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Log #3 on the fireplace mantel.</figcaption></div>

Six of fourteen done. **Chapter 3 — Returning Home** brings the hunt back to the neighborhood Michael grew up in.
