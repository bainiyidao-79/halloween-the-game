The last chapter of Michael's story, **Into the Shadows**, closes the collectible hunt with the final **three recordings**: Loomis Log #11, Laurie's recording, and Nurse Chambers's recording. Collect all three and the **Collector trophy** is yours.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch5-recordings.webp" alt="A dark bedroom scene in Into the Shadows in Halloween The Game" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Three recordings left, all in places Michael already knows.</figcaption></div>

## Before You Start

One preparation tip: **raise Bloodthirst to level 2 first** — that restores **Reality Tear**, which this chapter's rooftop-and-backyard route makes good use of.

## Loomis Log #11 — Back at the Wallace House

Return to the **Wallace House**, and specifically the **room where you hid Annie's body** in Chapter 4. The recording sits **in the corner by the window**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch5-recordings-1.webp" alt="The window corner holding Loomis Log 11 in the Wallace House" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Log #11 in the corner, by the window.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch5-recordings-2.webp" alt="The bedroom in the Wallace House in Into the Shadows" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The room the chapter sends you back to.</figcaption></div>

## Laurie's Recording — the Doyle House Side Table

Cross the street to the **Doyle house** — the same one you chased Laurie into at the end of Chapter 4.

In the dining area, look for the **small table beside the lamp**: Laurie's recording is on it.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch5-recordings-3.webp" alt="Laurie's recording on the side table by the lamp in the Doyle house" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Laurie's voice, on the lamp table.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch5-recordings-4.webp" alt="The Doyle house interior in Into the Shadows in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The Doyle house, one last visit.</figcaption></div>

## Nurse Chambers's Recording — the House at Number 1486

Head to the **far side of town**. On the right stands a house numbered **1486**.

Enter through the **front door and turn left** — the recording waits **on the bookshelf**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch5-recordings-5.webp" alt="The house at 1486 where Nurse Chambers's recording hides" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>1486 — front door, then left.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch5-recordings-6.webp" alt="Nurse Chambers's recording on the bookshelf inside house 1486" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The bookshelf holds the fourteenth and final recording.</figcaption></div>

That's **14 of 14** — all eleven Loomis Logs plus Sheriff Brackett's, Laurie's and Nurse Chambers's recordings — and the **Collector trophy** along with them. For the multiplayer side of the game, the [survivor beginner guide](/guides/survivor-beginner-guide) picks up where the story ends.
