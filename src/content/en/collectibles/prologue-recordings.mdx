Michael's story campaign hides **14 collectible recordings** across its prologue and five chapters — eleven Loomis Logs plus three from other voices — and finding all of them earns the **Collector trophy**. Two of them sit in the prologue, **Escape From Smith's Grove**: Loomis Log #2 and Loomis Log #4.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/prologue-recordings.webp" alt="The Smith's Grove courtyard at night where the first recording sits in Halloween The Game" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The prologue's courtyard — turn around before you run.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #2 — the Courtyard Trash Can

When the chapter starts, the game pushes you toward the exit. **Don't go.** Turn around instead and head back into the courtyard behind you.

Walk to the **back-left area**, and you will find the recording sitting **on top of a trash can**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/prologue-recordings-1.webp" alt="The courtyard trash can area in Escape From Smith's Grove" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The back-left corner of the courtyard — the trash can holds Log #2.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #4 — the Gate Shelter

Your escape route eventually leads to a large gate that needs lowering. When you reach it, **don't open it yet** — go **left** instead.

Just past the light you will see a **small shelter**. The recording waits inside, **on the shelf above the trash can**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/prologue-recordings-2.webp" alt="The shelter area near the gate in Escape From Smith's Grove" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Left of the gate, past the light — the shelter shelf holds Log #4.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/prologue-recordings-3.webp" alt="Recording pickup spot inside the gate shelter in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Log #4 on the shelf, exactly where the light doesn't reach.</figcaption></div>

Grab both, then walk out the gate and the prologue is done — two logs down, twelve to go. Chapter 1 keeps the hunt moving along the road to Haddonfield.
