Michael is back on home turf in **Chapter 3 — Returning Home**, and the neighborhood hides **two recordings**: Loomis Log #5 and Loomis Log #1.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch3-recordings.webp" alt="A residential street at night in Returning Home in Halloween The Game" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Home sweet home — two logs hidden on the old block.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #5 — the Pink Bedroom at Number 808

Push the story until you've killed the **intruder** and earned free map exploration. Then cross the street to the house at **number 808**.

Inside, look for the **pink bedroom** — the recording sits **on the desk**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch3-recordings-1.webp" alt="Approaching the house at 808 in Returning Home in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Number 808, straight across the street.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch3-recordings-2.webp" alt="The pink bedroom inside house 808 in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The pink bedroom — Log #5 on the desk.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch3-recordings-3.webp" alt="Recording pickup in the pink bedroom in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Grab it and move — the block is still crawling with witnesses.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #1 — the House Next to Marcus's

Open the map: **to the right of house 808** — next to Marcus's place — stands another **optional-entry house**. Go in.

**The recording sits on a cabinet in the middle of the house.**

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch3-recordings-4.webp" alt="The cabinet holding Loomis Log 1 in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Log #1 on the central cabinet — the first-numbered log is usually the last found.</figcaption></div>

Eight of fourteen collected, and the numbering is now complete from #1 through #11 except one. **Chapter 4 — The Babysitters** holds three recordings, including the first voice that isn't Dr. Loomis.
