**Chapter 4 — The Babysitters** is the richest chapter for collectors: **three recordings**, including the first non-Loomis voice of the set — Sheriff Brackett's.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch4-recordings.webp" alt="A Haddonfield house interior at night in The Babysitters chapter" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The Wallace House block hides three recordings this time.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #10 — the House Across From the Wallace House

Following the story brings you to the **Wallace House**. Directly **across from it** stands a house you can enter — number **1542**.

Inside, the recording waits **in the front hall**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch4-recordings-1.webp" alt="The house at 1542 across from the Wallace House in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>1542, right across from the Wallace House.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch4-recordings-2.webp" alt="Inside the front hall of house 1542 in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Log #10 in the front hall.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #7 — the Laundry Room Outhouse

On the way to the Wallace House a cutscene plays; when it ends you're in the **back yard**. Turn around and look **to the left**: a small **laundry room shed**.

**The recording sits on top of the washing machine** — and mind the dog. **Lester** has opinions about visitors.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch4-recordings-3.webp" alt="The laundry room shed in the back yard in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The laundry shed — watch for Lester the dog.</figcaption></div>

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch4-recordings-4.webp" alt="Loomis Log 7 on the washing machine in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Log #7 on the washer.</figcaption></div>

## Sheriff Brackett's Recording — the Doyle Dining Room

The chapter ends with Laurie bolting into the **Doyle house**, and you following her in.

Once inside, check the **dining room**: **the recording is on the dining table**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch4-recordings-5.webp" alt="Sheriff Brackett's recording on the dining table in the Doyle house" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Sheriff Brackett's voice, on the dining room table.</figcaption></div>

Eleven of fourteen — every Loomis Log from #1 to #11 is now accounted for. The finale holds the last three recordings, all from voices closest to Michael.
