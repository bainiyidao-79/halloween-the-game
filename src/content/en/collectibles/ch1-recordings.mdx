Michael steals Nurse Chambers's car and points it at Haddonfield in **Chapter 1 — The Road to Haddonfield**. The chapter hides **two recordings**: Loomis Log #6 and Loomis Log #8, both inside stops along the road.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch1-recordings.webp" alt="Inside the dive bar where Loomis Log 6 hides in Halloween The Game" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The dive bar's back hallway — follow it to the restroom door.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #6 — the Dive Bar Restroom

Inside the **"Red Rabbit" dive bar**, head toward the **restrooms at the back** of the building and go through to the end.

Open the **door on the left**: inside sits a woman — and next to her, **the recording on the dresser**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch1-recordings-1.webp" alt="The dresser inside the restroom room at the dive bar in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Log #6 on the dresser, in the room with the bar's only other patron.</figcaption></div>

## Loomis Log #8 — the Mechanic's Office

After leaving the bar, the story has you chase a rider. When the objective turns to going after the **mechanic**, hold off on the pursuit.

Enter his shop through the **front door**, then walk through into the **office**. Loomis Log #8 is **on the desk**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/ch1-recordings-2.webp" alt="The mechanic's office desk holding Loomis Log 8 in Halloween The Game" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The office desk — grab Log #8 before you deal with the mechanic.</figcaption></div>

That's both Chapter 1 logs — four of fourteen collected. Main Street is next, with two more waiting on the other side of town.
