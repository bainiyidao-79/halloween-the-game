Halloween: The Game tracks achievements and trophies on **PS5, Xbox Series, Steam and the Epic Store**, with a Platinum-class trophy ("The Shape") capping the list on PlayStation.

## How progression feeds your loadout

Match XP doesn't just level an account — it converts into **perk points** you spend in the loadout screen to roll, upgrade or scrap perks. Equipped perks are the ones most likely to appear during matches, so grinding XP directly strengthens your civilian builds. The story campaign also hides challenges (like Dr. Loomis' logs and context executions) that pay bonus XP.
