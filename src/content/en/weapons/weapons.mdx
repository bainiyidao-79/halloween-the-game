When Michael is breathing down your neck, you need something that creates distance — or something that hits hard enough to matter up close. The game gives survivors a mix of melee weapons and firearms; this ranking follows the community's launch-week tier list.

## Weapon tier list

| Tier | Weapons | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| **S** | Shotgun · Axe · Pickaxe | The shotgun's stopping power buys breathing room; the axe and pickaxe are the gold standard melee picks — and as Michael, heavy melee busts doors fast |
| **A** | Hunting Rifle · Cleaver | Rifle trades convenience for range; the cleaver is a strong, reliable middle-ground melee |
| **B** | Revolver · Hatchet · Kitchen Knife | Dependable but unremarkable — the iconic kitchen knife suffers from its short range |
| **C** | Pitchfork · Baseball Bat | Usable in a pinch; the pitchfork's reach looks scarier than it performs |

## Weapon rules to remember

- **Firearms vs melee**: guns keep Michael out of grab range, but ammo is limited — reload behind closed doors and don't waste shots during his execution animation (he's immune to damage then).
- **The pitchfork is throwable** — one of the few weapons with a ranged throw, and it's a funny way to finish a downed Michael.
- **Heavy melee as Michael** breaks doors faster than opening them.
- **Weapons dropped at escapes are gone forever** — if you're leaving through an exit, drop spare weapons next to it so NPCs or returning players can use them.
- **Give spare weapons to NPC civilians** (press R) — armed NPCs defend themselves and deal more damage.
