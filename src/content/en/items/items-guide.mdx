Everything that matters in a Haddonfield match passes through your inventory. Whether you are building an [escape](/guides/survivor-beginner-guide) or surviving a chase, this guide groups every item by the job it does.

## Escape-Critical Items

These unlock the exit routes — the win condition itself.

- **Repair kit** — the map's maintenance tool. Consumed repairing **vehicles, breaker boxes and phones**.
- **Car keys** — start a sedan, but only after it is **repaired and fueled**.
- **Gas can** — fuels the sedan and completes the drive-out escape.
- **Escape key** — opens the padlock on the main escape exit.
- **Fuse** — the mechanical gate's missing part. Cut the chain or metal seal off the door first.
- **Bolt cutters** — the swiss army knife: breaks padlocks on escapes **and** loot boxes (consumed on a loot box), and doubles as a melee weapon.
- **Police car keys** — unlock and start the police car; the trunk carries extra supplies.

The full unlock matrix per exit — which of these each escape actually needs — is in the [survivor guide](/guides/survivor-beginner-guide).

## Life-Savers (Grab-Breakers)

Used at the moment Michael's hands close on you. Mostly one-time consumables.

- **Butterfly knife** — one-time, breaks the grab instantly.
- **Pocket knife** — triggers a faster struggle prompt instead of an instant break.
- **Ice pick** — one-time instant break, same family as the butterfly knife.

## Lights, Distractions and Consumables

The high-frequency pocket slot: vision, distraction and recovery.

- **Flare gun** — stuns Michael and briefly marks the area.
- **Road flare** — blinds Michael **and can summon police support**.
- **Firecrackers** — a wall of light and noise that interrupts a chase.
- **Flashlight** — disorients Michael, pairs well with a melee swing, and stops him slipping away into the dark.
- **Flashlight batteries** — refills the light, because the dark always wins eventually.
- **Lockbox code** — opens a loot box without cutting anything.
- **Beer** — lowers fear at the price of a wobbly, swaying walk.
- **First aid spray / first aid kit** — restores health; the spray works mid-chase, the kit wants you standing still.
- **Soda** — a stamina top-up.
- **Smoke** — reads the area as ripples: Michael's movements, survivors, noise markers. Quiet map awareness in a canister.

## Throwables

- **Darts / baseball / rocks / bricks** — reusable throws that hurt Michael and smash windows; pick them back up after.
- **Cup** — same idea, single-use.

## Epic (Purple) Items

The rare end of the pool — limited spawns per match, game-swinging effects:

- **Ancient talisman** — blocks one attack or grab outright, blinds Michael with no struggle prompt; destroyed on trigger.
- **Brass lantern** — projects a green aura that lights up shadowed ground and **disables Michael's Shape Jump and stealth** inside it.
- **Hourglass** — heavy damage and a slow on Michael, extends the match timer, and speeds up all of your own interactions.

All three get a dedicated page with usage details: the [purple items guide](/items/purple-items).

## The Item Discipline That Ties It Together

Two habits convert a full inventory into wins. First, **route your looting around exits** — keys, fuses and fuel only matter near the escape they open. Second, **hoard your control items**: a flare gun thrown at nothing is the reason people die with a full bag. For squeezing XP out of the same looting loop, the [XP farming guide](/guides/civilian-xp-farming) has the numbers.
