Halloween: The Game doesn't teach you everything — several mechanics are never explained in-game. This hub gives you the fastest safe path into the game, then hands you off to the detailed guides.

## The recommended learning path

| Step | What to do | Why |
|------|------------|-----|
| 1 | Play **Michael's story** (single-player campaign) | Under 2 hours; introduces powers and controls one by one — useful for both sides |
| 2 | Read the [Beginner's Guide](/getting-started/beginners-guide) | Covers what the game never tells you: HUD, perks, escapes, items |
| 3 | Queue a **full 1v4 lobby** | 1v1/1v2/1v3 lobbies are brutal — wait for a full lobby |
| 4 | Keep the [Beginner FAQ](/getting-started/beginner-faq) handy | Fast answers to the questions the game ignores |

## Five rules for your first night

1. **Stalk before you stab (Michael)** — stalking raises a victim's stock; level 3 stock weakens them and makes kills far easier. Right-click zoom while stalking makes it much faster.
2. **Hunt context executions** — environmental kills (kitchens, bathrooms, poles, graves) give more bloodthirst and XP than plain attacks.
3. **Guard your bloodthirst** — the three-tier meter makes Michael hit harder and take less damage; you lose it by standing in light, taking damage, or getting knocked down.
4. **Pick your civilian wisely** — Eric has low athleticism and poor stamina; sturdier characters like Laurie take no damage through windows. Low-charisma characters get little from phoning the police.
5. **Set up your perk deck before queueing** — roll perks in the loadout screen, equip the good ones (e.g. stamina reducers), and scrap the junk; equipped perks are the ones that appear in your matches.
