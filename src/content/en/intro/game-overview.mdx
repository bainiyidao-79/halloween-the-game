This hub gathers everything on **Halloween: The Game**, the asymmetric horror game by IllFonic based on the 1978 *Halloween* film. On Halloween night, 1978, no one is safe from Michael Myers: you can play as the Boogeyman himself, or fight back against the face of evil as one of the Heroes of Haddonfield. The game also includes a standalone single-player story where you play Michael's side of that night.
