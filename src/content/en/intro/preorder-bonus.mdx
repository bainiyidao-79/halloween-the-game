One item covered every preorder of Halloween: The Game, in both editions: the exclusive **"Phantom" Michael Myers skin**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/intro/preorder-bonus.webp" alt="Michael Myers standing in a Haddonfield living room at night in Halloween The Game" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The Shape himself — the Phantom skin dresses him in pale blue.</figcaption></div>

## What the Phantom Skin Is

The Phantom look replaces Michael's standard boiler suit with a pale, ghost-blue version of the outfit — the killer rendered the way Haddonfield imagines him. It is a cosmetic: same moves, same [abilities](/guides/michael-guide), same fear factor.

## Who Got It

The rule was simple, and the store pages repeated it in both editions' listings:

- Preorder **Standard** → Phantom Michael skin
- Preorder **Deluxe** → Phantom Michael skin, plus the [rest of the Deluxe list](/intro/unlock-times)

That makes Phantom the one guaranteed item for early buyers, while the **"Inmate" skin, civilians Richard and Alexis, the Laurie Final Girl cosmetic and the digital comic** belonged to Deluxe alone.

## After Launch

Preorder windows closed with launch, so the skin's availability now depends on IllFonic bringing it back — publisher bonuses like this sometimes return as standalone or bundled cosmetics, sometimes do not. Everything else about playing Michael, skin aside, lives in the [Michael guide](/guides/michael-guide).
