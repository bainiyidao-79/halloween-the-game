Both editions of Halloween: The Game carry preorder bonuses, but they are not the same list. Here is the per-edition breakdown, together with the exact unlock windows the game shipped with.

## Unlock Times

- **Full launch:** September 9, 2026, 00:00 (East Asian time zones) — September 8 in Western regions
- **Early access:** September 5, 2026, 00:00 in the same time zones — four days ahead, [Deluxe Edition](/intro/game-content-overview) only

Platforms for both windows: PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam and Epic).

## Preorder Bonuses, Edition by Edition

**Standard Edition** — one bonus item:

- "Phantom" — Michael Myers exclusive skin

**Deluxe Edition** — the full stack:

- "Phantom" — Michael Myers exclusive skin
- "Inmate" — Michael Myers exclusive skin
- Richard — exclusive civilian character
- Alexis — exclusive civilian character
- Laurie — Final Girl cosmetic
- Digital comic

## What That Means in Practice

The Phantom skin is the universal [preorder bonus](/intro/preorder-bonus): it came with every preorder regardless of edition. Everything else on the list — the Inmate skin, both civilians, the Laurie cosmetic and the comic — is what the extra cost of Deluxe actually bought.

Two of the bonuses affect gameplay identity directly: Richard and Alexis are **playable civilians**, not just outfits, so Deluxe owners had two extra faces to bring into Haddonfield from night one. If you are new to the game instead, the [survivor beginner guide](/guides/survivor-beginner-guide) applies no matter which characters you own.
