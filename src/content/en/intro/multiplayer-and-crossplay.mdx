Halloween: The Game's multiplayer is a **1 vs 4 asymmetric match**: one player stalks Haddonfield as Michael Myers while four players try to protect the town's civilians and escape the night alive. Matches are online PvP with co-op elements on the civilian side, with full crossplay between PC, PS5 and Xbox after launch.

## The match at a glance

| Element | Michael's view | Civilians' view |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| Special targets (golden) | 3 key civilians worth the most points — always NPCs | Protect and evacuate them first |
| Regular civilians (blue) | 5 more NPCs worth fewer points | Rescue, arm them, hide them |
| The 4 players | Hunt them — but they're not the main objective | Survive, save others, escape last if you can |
| Win condition | Score 3,000+ points | More civilians (and players) saved than killed |

Several useful details the game doesn't explain clearly:

- **Special targets are always NPCs** — the golden pillars of light and the "golden" noise pings mark where they are.
- **Dead or escaped players can come back**: after dying or escaping, a player may return as a **police officer** (when "police respond" is available) or, once the radio is enabled, as **Dr. Loomis** — both can work to detain Michael.
- **Escapes** come in several forms: ground hatches, a broken-down car (repair kit + gas can + sedan key, then drive out of town), a police wagon (after enough phone calls), an exit gate at the edge of the map (bolt cutters or padlock key), and storm cellars. Each exit has a limited number of uses.
- A match scored **3,000+ points is a win for Michael**; the civilian team wins on points if they save more than Michael kills.

## Crossplay status

The game shipped with **crossplay between PC, PS5 and Xbox Series**, but it was unreliable at launch. **Patch 1.0.1 fixed crossplay** and also buffed Michael — community coverage of the patch landed within the first week after release. If you're seeing connection errors, make sure everyone has updated to at least 1.0.1.

## Matchmaking tip

Don't queue into a 1v1, 1v2 or 1v3 lobby — the game is balanced around a **full 1v4**. Wait for the lobby to fill before readying up, and mute anyone rude from the scoreboard.
