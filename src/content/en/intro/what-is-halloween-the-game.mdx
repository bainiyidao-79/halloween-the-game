**Halloween: The Game** is an asymmetric horror game by IllFonic based on the 1978 *Halloween* film. On Halloween night, 1978, no one is safe from Michael Myers: you can take The Shape of Evil and terrorize the residents of Haddonfield as Michael, or arm yourself with weapons and everyday household items as one of the Heroes of Haddonfield, alert the authorities, and rescue innocent families. A standalone single-player story also lets you experience "The Night He Came Home" through Michael's eyes.

## The two ways to play

| Mode | You play | The other side |
|------|----------|----------------|
| Asymmetric multiplayer | Michael Myers (1 player) | Up to 4 players as Heroes of Haddonfield — with Haddonfield civilians (NPCs) to rescue and protect |
| Single-player story | Michael Myers | Story-driven: a prologue followed by five chapters (~2 hours) |

The game is a horror sandbox: dynamic map features, life-like NPC interactions, and player abilities on both sides. Michael stalks from the shadows and grows stronger as his bloodthirst rises; civilians search for escape routes, arm themselves, call the police, and can eventually return as cops — or even Dr. Loomis — to take Michael down.

|------|--------|

| Steam reviews | "Mostly Positive" — about 70% positive (6,069 positive / 2,580 negative at launch week) |
| Players milestone | 1 million players announced on the official site within launch week |

Reviewers and players describe the multiplayer as easy to win as Michael if you get a good start, while civilians rely on teamwork, stealth, and knowing the escape spawns. The single-player story is short and simple — it works best as a tutorial for Michael's powers.

## Is it based on the 1978 movie?

Yes — the game is set directly after the events of the original film, and the Halloween and Michael Myers trademarks are held by Compass International Pictures. Gun Interactive (publisher of asymmetric horror games) co-publishes.
