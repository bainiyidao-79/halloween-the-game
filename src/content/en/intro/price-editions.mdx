Two editions, two price points — Halloween: The Game keeps its store page simple. Here is what each version cost at launch and what the price difference buys.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/intro/price-editions.webp" alt="Halloween The Game standard edition pre-order artwork with masked Michael" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The Standard Edition: the whole game, no add-ons.</figcaption></div>

## The Two Price Points

| Edition | US Steam | China Steam | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | $39.99 | ¥137 | Base game |
| **Deluxe** | $59.99 | ¥200 | Base game + early access + bonus content |

Steam sets prices per region, so the exact number on your store page follows your local currency. The two figures above are the reference points from the US and China storefronts.

## Is Deluxe Worth the Difference?

The $20 / ¥63 gap buys exactly the [Deluxe bonus list](/intro/unlock-times): four days of [early access](/intro/release-date), the "Inmate" Michael skin on top of the preorder "Phantom" skin, exclusive civilians **Richard** and **Alexis**, the Laurie Final Girl cosmetic, and a digital comic with unrevealed post-launch content to come.

For players who were going to main Michael anyway, two extra killer skins plus four early days is the argument. For everyone else, the [Standard Edition](/intro/game-content-overview) contains the complete game — the full 1v4 sandbox, the story campaign, and every map.

One more cost-of-ownership note: the preorder [Phantom skin bonus](/intro/preorder-bonus) was tied to preordering, not to edition, so Standard preorders did not miss out on it.
