Halloween: The Game released on **September 8, 2026** for PC (Steam), PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Players who bought the **Digital Deluxe Edition** got in four days early, on September 4.

## Release summary

| Question | Answer (verified Sep 15, 2026) |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| Release date | September 8, 2026 |
| Early access | September 4 for Digital Deluxe owners |
| Platforms | PC (Steam), PS5, Xbox Series X\|S |
| PS4 version | Not announced in any source we checked |
| Price | Paid title — Steam China lists ¥137; check your region's store for local pricing |
| Physical copy | Yes — a physical Collector's Edition for PS5 is sold through major retailers (Best Buy), plus standard physical stock |
| Demo | No public demo; a pre-launch beta ran before release |
| Game Pass | Not announced in any source we checked |

## Editions

| Edition | What we know |
|---------|--------------|
| Standard | Base game |
| Digital Deluxe | Base game + 4-day early access (September 4) |
| Collector's Edition | Physical PS5 edition sold at retail (Best Buy listing) |

## Pre-order and beta notes

Pre-orders ran through the official pre-order page (halloweengame.com/news/preorder), the Xbox and PlayStation digital stores, and major retailers before launch. The pre-launch beta has ended — the game is now fully released ("NOW AVAILABLE" on the official site), so there is no separate beta client to download anymore.

If you're deciding whether to buy, start with [What Is Halloween: The Game?](/intro/what-is-halloween-the-game) and the current state of [patches and updates](/news/patch-notes-and-roadmap).
