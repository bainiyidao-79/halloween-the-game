Halloween: The Game runs on Unreal Engine 5 across a fully simulated Haddonfield, and its PC spec list reads like it. One requirement is non-negotiable on both tiers: the game **must be installed on an SSD or NVMe drive**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/intro/system-requirements.webp" alt="A dark Haddonfield house exterior at night in Halloween The Game" loading="eager" /><figcaption>All of Haddonfield lives on your drive — 45 GB of it, SSD required.</figcaption></div>

## Minimum Requirements (1080p @ 30 fps)

- **OS:** Windows 11 64-bit (64-bit processor and OS required)
- **CPU:** Intel LGA 1200+ / AMD AM4+ (Zen 2+)
- **RAM:** 16 GB
- **GPU:** GTX 1660+ / RX 590+ / Arc A770+
- **DirectX:** Version 12
- **Network:** Broadband internet connection
- **Storage:** 45 GB available space
- **Additional note:** SSD/NVMe required

## Recommended Requirements (4K @ 30 fps)

- **OS:** Windows 11 64-bit (64-bit processor and OS required)
- **CPU:** Intel LGA 1700+ / AMD AM5+
- **RAM:** 32 GB
- **GPU:** RTX 3000 series / RX 7000 series
- **DirectX:** Version 12
- **Network:** Broadband internet connection
- **Storage:** 45 GB available space
- **Additional note:** SSD/NVMe required

## What the Specs Tell You

Three things stand out. First, **Windows 11 across the board** — Windows 10 is not on the list at all. Second, the recommended tier targets **4K at 30 fps**, consistent with a game that leans on dense, interactable suburban neighborhoods rather than raw frame rate. Third, the **45 GB install must live on an SSD** — budget the drive space before launch night, not during it.

Meeting the minimum spec means playable multiplayer at 1080p30. Since [matches](/guides/survivor-beginner-guide) put eight characters, dynamic weather and roaming NPC civilians on one map, staying above the floor helps.
