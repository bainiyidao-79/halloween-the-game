The 1978 Halloween night is the setting, and the launch calendar was built to match it. Halloween: The Game — IllFonic's asymmetric horror sandbox set directly after the film — headed to store on **September 8, 2026**, right in front of the holiday itself.

## Launch Date and Platforms

The game released on **September 8, 2026** (September 9, 00:00 in East Asian time zones) across:

- **PlayStation 5**
- **Xbox Series X|S**
- **PC via Steam**
- **PC via Epic Games Store**

## Early Access for Deluxe Owners

Buying the [Deluxe Edition](/intro/game-content-overview) moved the date up: **early access opened on September 4, 2026** — four full days before everyone else. That window is the main functional difference between the two editions, alongside the extra skins and civilians.

## Preorders

Preorders opened well before launch and stayed up through release week. Every preorder, in either edition, carried the ["Phantom" Michael Myers skin](/intro/preorder-bonus) as the bonus.
