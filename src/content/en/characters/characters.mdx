Haddonfield's cast splits into two playable sides — **Michael Myers** and the **Heroes of Haddonfield** — plus a town full of NPC civilians that both sides fight over.

## Michael Myers

The playable killer of the multiplayer mode and the protagonist of the story campaign. Known in-game and in the film as The Shape, The Boogeyman, The Babysitter Killer and Evil on Two Legs. His full profile and power kit are on the [Michael Myers page](/characters/michael-myers).

## Character systems to know

- **Personal perks**: press T on the character select screen — each character has perks that are always applied when you play them.
- **Stats** (athleticism, charisma, etc.): the game never explains them. Community findings so far: very low charisma makes phoning the police nearly useless; low athleticism ruins stamina and window dives.
- **Loadout**: beyond appearance you can set a **starting item** and edit your **perk deck** — equipped perks are the ones most likely to appear during matches.
- **Returning roles**: after dying or escaping, players can return as cops (RNG once "police respond" is available) or as Dr. Loomis (guaranteed once the radio is enabled after a death).
