**Michael Audrey Myers** — The Shape, The Boogeyman, The Babysitter Killer, Evil on Two Legs, The Shape of Evil — is the playable killer of Halloween: The Game. Born October 1957, he is 21 years old during the game's 1978 setting, having spent 15 of those years locked in Smith's Grove Sanitarium for the murder of his sister Judith.

## His power kit

| Power | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| Stalking / Killer Sense | Build stock on victims (levels 1–3); level 3 weakens them, marks them longer, and makes kills easier. Zoom (right-click) while stalking tracks much faster |
| Shape Jump | Become an invisible shadow — only in darkness and out of line of sight. Passes through unlit doors; highlights special-target locations |
| Shape Dash | Three dash charges while in shape jump for rapid repositioning |
| Detection Pulse | Sends out a pulse that briefly outlines victims in a radius; victims inside structures get their building marked instead |
| Evil Presence | A story-to-multiplayer ability that pressures nearby victims |
| Blackout | Kills all lights, flashlights and vehicles in the vicinity — temporarily |
| Reality Tear | A grab that triggers a number-sequence mini-game; during executions you are immune to damage |
| Bloodthirst | Three-tier meter: more damage dealt, less taken, weapon executions unlocked — lost in light, from damage, or when knocked down |

## Executions

- **Context executions** — environmental kills (kitchens, bathrooms, poles, graves, fire, windows) for bonus XP and bloodthirst
- **Bloodthirsty executions** — unlocked at high bloodthirst tiers
- **Weapon executions** — the mini-game finisher with your equipped weapon

## Playing him well

Stalk before every kill, hunt the golden special targets, cut phone lines before victims call the police, blackout before entering lit areas, and never brawl in the open against flashlights. The full playbook is in the [Strategy Guide](/guides/strategy-guide).

## Customization

The game includes Michael customization and skins (documented on the community wiki's Michael Customization and Michael Myers Skins pages), and a **streamer mode** replaces the iconic theme with a copyright-free version.
